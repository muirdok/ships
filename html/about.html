About this project

Why we track the largest ships ever built

Giants of the Sea is a small reference site that compares the dimensions, tonnage and capacity of the largest vessels ever constructed — from the 458-metre Seawise Giant to the newest ultra-large container ships and floating cities now crossing the oceans.

What we cover

  • Length records — the longest ships ever built and afloat today.
  • Container capacity — how many twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU) modern boxships can carry.
  • Gross tonnage and deadweight — the two common ways the shipping industry measures size.
  • Passenger ships — the largest cruise vessels by gross tonnage and passenger count.
  • Warships — the largest aircraft carriers and their displacement.
  • Floating facilities — structures such as the Prelude FLNG which never enter port.

Sources

Figures are compiled from publicly available maritime registries, classification societies (Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas), operator disclosures and Guinness World Records. Where records are close or disputed, we state both figures and note the difference.

Data download

The full dataset is available as a plain-text file: /download/ships.txt. It is regenerated whenever a new record holder enters service or an older ship is broken up.
