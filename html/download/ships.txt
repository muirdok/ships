Record holders by category
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Seawise Giant (ULCC) — LONGEST SHIP EVER BUILT
   Length:      458.45 m (1,504 ft)
   Beam:        68.8 m
   Deadweight:  564,763 DWT
   Gross tons:  260,941 GT
   Built:       1979 (Oppama, Japan)
   Scrapped:    2010 (Alang, India)
   Notes:       Largest deadweight tonnage ever recorded.
                Sank during Iran-Iraq war, later refloated
                and renamed "Happy Giant", "Jahre Viking",
                then "Knock Nevis" before demolition.

2. MSC Irina (Container Ship) — LARGEST CONTAINER SHIP
   Length:      399.9 m
   Beam:        61.3 m
   Capacity:    24,346 TEU
   Gross tons:  233,000 GT
   Built:       2023 (Hudong-Zhonghua, China)
   Operator:    Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC)
   Notes:       Part of MSC's 24,000+ TEU fleet. Uses
                dual-fuel LNG-ready propulsion.

3. Legend of the Seas (Cruise Ship) — LARGEST CRUISE SHIP
   Length:      ~365 m
   Beam:        ~66 m
   Gross tons:  248,660 GT
   Passengers:  5,600 (double) / 7,600 (max)
   Crew:        ~2,350
   Decks:       20
   Built:       2025 (Meyer Turku, Finland)
   Operator:    Royal Caribbean International
   Class:       Icon class
   Notes:       Sister to Icon of the Seas and Utopia of
                the Seas. Features 7 pools and the largest
                waterpark at sea.

4. USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78) — LARGEST WARSHIP
   Length:      333 m
   Beam:        78 m (flight deck)
   Displacement: ~100,000 t
   Aircraft:    75+ fixed & rotary wing
   Propulsion:  2 x A1B nuclear reactors
   Crew:        ~4,500
   Built:       2017 (Newport News, USA)
   Operator:    United States Navy
   Notes:       Most advanced aircraft carrier ever built.
                Capable of 220 sorties per day, EMALS
                catapults, advanced arresting gear.

5. Prelude FLNG (Floating Facility) — LARGEST FLOATING OBJECT
   Length:      488 m
   Beam:        74 m
   Displacement: ~600,000 t (fully loaded)
   Gross tons:  ~403,000 GT
   Built:       2017 (Samsung Heavy Industries, S. Korea)
   Operator:    Shell
   Location:    Browse Basin, Australia
   Notes:       Not self-propelled. Extracts, liquefies,
                stores and offloads LNG at sea. Never
                entered port under its own power.

6. Pioneering Spirit (Heavy Lift / Pipelay)
   Length:      382 m
   Beam:        124 m
   Gross tons:  ~403,342 GT
   Built:       2016 (Daewoo Shipbuilding, S. Korea)
   Operator:    Allseas
   Notes:       World's largest construction vessel.
                Can lift 48,000 t topsides in a single
                lift. Dismantled Shell Brent Delta.

7. Mozah (Q-Max LNG Carrier)
   Length:      345 m
   Beam:        53.8 m
   Capacity:    266,000 m³ LNG
   Built:       2008 (Samsung Heavy Industries)
   Operator:    Nakilat
   Notes:       First Q-Max vessel. Largest LNG carrier
                class in regular operation.

8. RMS Titanic (Historical Reference)
   Length:      269 m
   Beam:        28 m
   Gross tons:  46,328 GT
   Built:       1912 (Harland & Wolff, Belfast)
   Notes:       Largest ship afloat when launched.
                Sank on maiden voyage, 15 April 1912.

============================================================
  LEGEND
------------------------------------------------------------
  ULCC = Ultra Large Crude Carrier
  TEU  = Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit (container)
  GT   = Gross Tonnage (internal volume)
  DWT  = Deadweight Tonnage (cargo + fuel + crew)
  LNG  = Liquefied Natural Gas
  FLNG = Floating Liquefied Natural Gas
